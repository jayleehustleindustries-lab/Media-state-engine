        QUALITY FLOOR
        
The no-distortion standard

        

          
Image integrity
At least 1080 px on the short edge and 2 MP. No resize or crop is applied. Under-spec images are blocked.

          
Lighting + depth
Soft, even front/45° key light; clean separation from the background; natural skin tone; shoulders and upper torso visible.

          
Symmetry + identity
Eye-level camera, no wide-angle selfie lens, centered head, visible jawline/forehead/eyes/mouth, and a neutral expression for the primary anchor.

          
Best frame
Vertical 4:5 to 9:16, single person, uncluttered background. Add a clean 3/4 and full-body reference in the extra lines for planned Looks.

        

        
What the system does—and does not—guarantee
